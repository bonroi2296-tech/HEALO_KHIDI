## 📋 Brief (copy this)

**Brand:** healwith (always lowercase) — a medical-tourism concierge connecting
Russian / Central-Asian cancer patients with top Korean hospitals.

**Feeling:** calm, trustworthy, public-health, warm and human — NOT cold or
clinical. A patient and a serious-minded clinician should both look at it and
feel "safe, premium, credible." Avoid: syringes, 3D, stock medical imagery,
literal medical cross, literal heart.

**Mark:** a lowercase **"h"** monogram with a *custom letterform* (not a default
font). The brand name means **"heal WITH"** — being beside the patient, healing
together. Bake that idea into the form (subtle, abstract, elegant): companionship,
shelter, continuity of care, or quiet recovery/growth. The concept should live in
the shape itself, not as a tacked-on icon.

**Style:** flat vector, geometric, high contrast, beautifully balanced. Must stay
legible down to **48px**. No gradients, no drop shadows, no serif, no thin
hairlines, no text other than the "h".

**Color:**
- Primary teal `#0D9488`
- Deliver both: **white mark on teal** AND **teal mark on white**
- Optional secondary accent: slate `#475569`

**Layout / delivery:**
- Square 1024×1024 canvas; mark inside central **80% safe zone** with even padding
  (survives Android rounded-squircle masking).
- Files: **SVG** (vector source) + 1024 PNG, transparent-bg mark + solid-teal version.
- Launcher sizes if possible: 512 / 192 / 48.

**Deliverables:** 2–3 distinct directions, then refine the chosen one.
