## EN — Partnership Proposal

**healwith — Korea Cancer Care Concierge for International Patients**

Dear [Agency Name],

healwith is a Korean government–supported (KHIDI) ICT platform that connects
international cancer patients with Korea's leading oncology hospitals. We provide
**remote video pre-consultation, real-time interpretation in 6 languages
(RU/KZ/EN/ZH/JA/KO), cost estimates, visa support, and structured post-care** —
the full journey from diagnosis to follow-up.

**Why partner with us**
- **You refer, we handle the hard parts.** Interpretation, video pre-consultation
  with Korean oncologists, cost estimates, and follow-up are managed on one platform.
- **Real-time case visibility.** Your dedicated agency portal shows the live status
  of every patient you refer — no more "waiting in the dark" for slow hospital replies.
- **Integrated care path.** Partner tertiary hospitals (Ewha Seoul, Ewha Mokdong,
  Korea University Guro, Severance) for surgery/chemo + Myeonryeok Korean-medicine
  hospitals for immune support & rehabilitation.
- **Commission sharing.** Transparent referral commission per admitted patient.
  *(Details: [PO 확정] — proposed structure below.)*

**What we ask from you**
- Refer cancer patients considering treatment abroad.
- Share basic case information (with patient consent) so we can prepare consultation.

**Next step:** A 30-minute video call to align on commission terms and process.

Contact: [PO 연락처 — 이메일/WhatsApp/Telegram]

Уважаемые партнёры [Название агентства],

которая соединяет иностранных онкологических пациентов с ведущими онкологическими

**Что мы просим от вас**

## 수수료 구조 (PO 방침 2026-06-20)

> **PO 방침**: healwith가 받는 유치수수료(치료비 10~25%)에서 **그 수수료의 30~50%를 에이전시에 분배.** **협의 가능.**
> ※ "치료비의 30~50%"가 아니라 **"우리 수수료의 30~50%"** — 헷갈리지 말 것.

**계산 예시** (치료비 2천만원, 우리 유치수수료 20% = 400만원 가정):
| 에이전시 분배율 | 에이전시 받는 돈 | healwith 몫 |
|---|---|---|
| 30% | 120만원 | 280만원 |
| 40% | 160만원 | 240만원 |
| 50% | 200만원 | 200만원 |

**지급 방식 (추천)**: 노쇼 리스크 분산 위해 **유치 확정 시 절반 + 치료 완료 시 절반**.
첫 거래는 소액 고정(건당 USD)으로 신뢰 먼저 쌓고 정률 전환 권장. **최종 % 는 에이전시별 협의.**

정합 맞춰야 함. 수수료 실수령 경로는 PO·회계 확정 사항.
