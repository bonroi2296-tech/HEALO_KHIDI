3. Conditions

3-1. Cost to the patient

  • The facilitation fee is paid by the medical institution to BONROI, not by the patient. Under Article 2(1) of the Integrated Notice on Support for Overseas Expansion of Healthcare and Attraction of Foreign Patients, the facilitation fee is a payment from the medical institution to the facilitator. It is never added to the amount you are billed.
  • You pay the medical institution directly for treatment, according to the estimate the institution issues to you.
  • Any optional add-on service that would be charged to you (for example, a paid airport pickup) is agreed with you in writing in advance. No charge is applied without your prior written agreement.

3-2. Before the treatment contract

  • The attracting medical institution must provide you with a treatment contract and an estimate of expected treatment costs before the treatment contract is concluded (Article 8(2)2 of the Act). Please make sure you receive and read it.
  • Cost figures shown on our website or in chat are reference ranges. Only the estimate issued by the medical institution is binding.

3-3. Limits of what we can promise

  • We do not guarantee any medical outcome, cure, or recovery period.
  • Treatment eligibility is decided by the medical institution's physicians, not by us.
  • Scheduling may change for medical or administrative reasons; we will inform you as soon as we know.

3-4. Cancellation

  • You may withdraw from our facilitation service at any time, at no cost, by contacting admin@healwith.co.kr.
  • Cancellation of treatment itself, and any refund of amounts already paid to the medical institution, follow that institution's own policy. We will assist you in communicating the request.

Protection of Health and Personal Information

Posted pursuant to Article 8(1) of the Act on Support for Overseas Expansion of Healthcare and Attraction of Foreign Patients, and Article 7(1)3 of its Enforcement Rule. Governed by the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea (PIPA).

1. What we collect and why

Travel and stayPassport details, visa status, arrival plan, accommodation — for visa documentation and arrival support

2. Legal basis

  • Health information is sensitive data under Article 23 of PIPA. We process it only with your separate, explicit consent.
  • Transfer of your information outside Korea requires your separate consent under Article 28-8 of PIPA. We tell you the recipient, the country, the purpose, and the retention period before you decide.
  • You may refuse any consent. Refusing consent for non-essential purposes does not affect the core service.

3. Who we share it with

  • The medical institution you choose — only what is needed for your consultation and treatment.
  • An overseas partner agency, only where you asked for local support and only with your consent.
  • We never sell personal information and never share it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

  • Health information (sensitive data): kept only for as long as needed to provide the service — pre-consultation, treatment support and follow-up. It is destroyed without delay when you close your account or withdraw consent.
  • Account information: kept while your membership is active; destroyed within 30 days of withdrawal. Accounts inactive for 3 years are destroyed after prior notice.
  • Records required by Korean law are kept for the statutory period — for example, records of consumer complaints and dispute handling: 3 years (Article 6 of the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce).
  • Your medical records themselves are held by the medical institution, not by us, and are kept for 10 years under Article 22 of the Medical Service Act. We do not hold copies of those originals.
  • Destruction is irreversible: electronic files are permanently deleted, printed material is shredded.

5. How we protect it

  • Health information and identifying details are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • All connections use TLS. Access is restricted by role and every access is logged.
  • Staff handling your data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

6. Your rights

  • You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing, and you may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Send the request to admin@healwith.co.kr. We act within 10 days.
  • Exercising these rights never disadvantages you.
